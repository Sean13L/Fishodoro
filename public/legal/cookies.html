Cookie Policy

Effective 4 November 2026.

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies, and nothing on this site loads from a third party. Here's everything Fishodoro stores in your browser.

These make the service work, so they don't need your consent.

Preferences (your choice)

Stored only if you choose "Allow preferences". Nothing leaves your device.

NameTypePurposeLasts
fish.prefsLocal storageYour guest look, sound and focus settingsUntil you clear it or choose "Essential only"
fish.guestLocal storageFish you caught as a guestUntil you clear it or choose "Essential only"

If you choose "Essential only", guest settings and guest fish last until you reload the page. Signed-in players' settings and fish are saved to their account as part of the service.

Changing your choice

Use "Cookie settings" in the footer of the main page at any time. Choosing "Essential only" removes stored preferences and guest fish from this device.
