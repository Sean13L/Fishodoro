What we don't collect

No name, no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no tracking pixels. Your IP address isn't stored in our database. Your time zone isn't sent to us: the Adaptive and city skies use your device's clock. We don't sell your data.

Guests

If you don't create an account, your fish and settings stay on your device, and only if you allow preferences. Our servers receive ordinary web requests but store nothing about you.

Who else receives data

  • Brevo sends our security emails, so it receives your email address and the message.
  • Vercel runs the app and handles web requests, and may keep short-lived technical logs.
  • Turso stores the database.

We stay responsible for the data we give these providers and use contracts to require comparable protection.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct your personal information, ask what we do with it, and withdraw your consent.

  • Download your data: Account → Download my data.
  • Delete your data: Account → Delete my account. This permanently removes everything we hold about you straight away, apart from copies in backups, which expire on their normal schedule.
  • Can't sign in? Email {{OPERATOR_EMAIL}} from your account's address to request access or deletion. We'll respond within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. If you live outside Canada, you may have other rights and a local authority you can contact.

Children

Accounts are for people aged 16 and over. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we'll delete it. Younger players can use guest mode, which stores nothing about them on our servers.

Security

Passwords are hashed with Argon2id, connections use HTTPS, session cookies can't be read by scripts, and two-factor authentication is available. No system is perfectly secure, so if a breach creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we'll notify you and the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as soon as we can.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and tell account holders before the change applies.
